The Problem · The School
Page (N°003)

A school is a business
being run out of
exercise books.

Private schools across the region collect fees worth billions of shillings a year and account for almost none of it in a system. The head teacher cannot see today what the school is owed.
  • Nobody agrees on the arrears figure. Fees live in a ledger book and three spreadsheets. Ask the bursar, the head and the board, and you get three numbers.
  • Parents hear about absence at the end of term. By then the child has missed weeks and the school has lost the chance to act.
  • Report cards take weeks of evenings. Marks are copied, totalled and ranked by hand, every term, for every stream.
  • Payroll and PAYE are computed manually. The mistake is found by the tax authority, not by the school.
  • The software that exists bills per student and sells messaging and AI as extras, so a growing school pays more every year.
The Problem · The Learning
Page (N°004)

The school runs on paper, so the people around the child work blind: the teacher plans alone, the parent finds out too late, and the holidays are lost.

~3

Learning stops unless a family can pay for holiday tuition. Parents already spend on it. Most of it is unstructured and nobody measures whether it worked.

Edtech that sells straight to parents has to win millions of families one at a time. Reach has been possible; getting paid has been the hard part.

Why We Win
Page (N°011)

AI native, not AI assisted.

Most school software adds a chatbot on top. Atlas was designed around the agent from the first migration, which is why it can be trusted with a school's money and its children.
01

It cannot invent a number

Money figures come from reporting functions reconciled to the ledger. The model is never allowed to do the arithmetic.

02

It cannot leak

The model reaches data only through a fixed tool catalogue, permission checked on every call, with the school identity taken from the server.

03

It cannot act alone

Every change runs propose, confirm, execute. A person confirms, and the confirm step is not reachable by the model.

04

It owns the record

A learning app can copy content. It cannot copy five years of a school's ledger, marks and parent relationships.

A tutor or a planning assistant inside a school plan would have broken the margin two years ago. Today the whole AI bill for a school is under US$40 a year.

Mobile money and near universal phone ownership mean a parent can hear what is owed, and pay it, in the same hour.

In Tanzania the incumbent has about 490 schools after a decade, under one in ten. In Kenya, Zeraki proved schools adopt fast: from 400 to 5,800 schools in three years.
